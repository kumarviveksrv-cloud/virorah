Privacy

virorah.com is the company site of Virorah Private Limited, Gurugram, Haryana, India. Virorah Private Limited is the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 for any personal data this site processes.

This site has no accounts, sign-up, forms or uploads. We do not ask you for your name, email or any other personal information here. If you email us at info@virorah.com, we use your message only to reply to it.

The typefaces and the 3D graphics library are served from virorah.com itself, so your browser does not contact Google or a content network to load them. We do not run advertising, the Meta Pixel or any other ad-tracking technology, and we do not sell your data.

Consent in Europe

If your browser's timezone is in Europe, including the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity do not load until you press Accept on the banner. Declining keeps them off. We remember your choice for 12 months in your browser. Region is judged from your browser's timezone, not your exact location, so if you are in Europe and see no banner, or the reverse, you can still open it below. Elsewhere, analytics load without a banner and you can opt out as described next.

How to opt out

  • Microsoft Clarity: open any page of this site with ?noclarity=1 at the end of the address, for example virorah.com/?noclarity=1. Your browser remembers the choice.
  • Google Analytics: block it with your browser's cookie settings, a tracking blocker, or Google's opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023 you can ask us to access, correct or erase personal data we hold about you, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Because analytics data is generally not linked to your name or email, we may not be able to identify records as yours. We will still look into any request. Write to info@virorah.com; we aim to respond within 48 hours.
