financial statement suite.
Five intelligence pillars that translate every people decision into the language your CEO and CFO already speak. Each pillar is a live intelligence tool that builds on the last. Together they form something no HR team has ever had: a full financial picture of their people.
Five pillars. One fluency.
L1 — Foundation
The Value Ledger
Every HR decision has a financial entry. Most organisations just never wrote it down. The Value Ledger is where you start: connecting every people action to a financial consequence for the first time.
→
L2 — Talent
The Talent Premium
What is the financial value of having better-than-average people? L2 names it. Quantify the premium that high performers generate and the cost of settling for average across any role or function.
→
L3 — Culture
Org Vitals
You’ve been calling it culture. It’s your organisation’s DNA, named and measured. Org Vitals surfaces the financial health signals hidden inside your engagement, attrition, and performance data.
→
L4 — Profit & Loss
The Human P&L
HR has always had a P&L. Nobody taught them to read it. L4 builds HR’s income statement — revenue from people quality, cost from people failures, and the net that results from every HR decision made this year.
→
L5 — Balance Sheet
The Human Balance Sheet
Your organisation’s human capital balance sheet. It has always existed. Nobody ever filed it. L5 does exactly that: assets on one side (talent, trust, culture), liabilities on the other (attrition risk, skill debt, disengagement drag).
